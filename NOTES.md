## Dead ends (so nobody tries them twice)
12:08 · Dan's agent · Census (count distinct objects by listing per shard + Decision 1 "same object?" merges) loses to plain Hydrogen 5-vote: EM 0.30 vs 0.567 on 30 SVCBench questions. Decision 1 can't tell look-alike instances apart from crops: it never merges identical ceiling lights (0/109 pairs) yet merges different chairs (6 → 1). Hydrogen on the whole clip at low media resolution already counts well (MAE 0.8, about 4k tokens per minute of video).

## Findings
11:40 · Dan's agent · Decision 1 works on video only via `api_version="v1beta"` and model `models/gemini-decision-1` (v1 answers 404, and `models.list` doesn't show it). One 25 s 426x240 clip costs about 2k tokens, 0.8-1.9 s, with the same answer on repeat. On 39 real robot clips it has no success bias: every failure scored p ≤ 0.42, and its errors are successes scored too low (8 of 20 under 0.5 in one arm or the other). Thresholds need calibrating; adding more questions didn't help (0.80 vs 0.875). 1 of 40 clips was blocked by the content filter for no visible reason.

- 12:35 Video generation, measured with `spikes/s5_videogen.py` (one run each, media in `.context/videogen/`): Omni 1.2 Flash is one blocking `client.interactions.create(model="gemini-omni-1.2-flash", input=..., response_format={"type":"video","duration":"4s"})` call, nothing to poll; the mp4 comes back as base64 in `output_video.data`. Text to video 4 s: 23.5 s wall. Image to video 4 s: 26.3 s. Edit of an uploaded 12.3 s ego2act clip ("make the bottle red"): 131.9 s, and the hand's actions matched the source in the 3 frames compared. Extend the same clip by 3 s with a prompted action: 92.0 s. All outputs 1280x720, 24 fps, h264 + 48 kHz AAC.
- Omni limits (docs): 3-15 s per clip, input video for editing 15 s or less, extension 3-10 s per turn up to 30 s total, inline delivery up to about 4 MB (above that use `delivery: "uri"`), no system prompt or temperature, SynthID watermark. An edit is too slow for a live demo beat (about 2 min); a 4 s generation fits (about 25 s). No content-filter refusals seen in 4 calls.
- Genie 4 works from Python: `client.aio.interactions.create(model="genie-4")` then `client.aio.interactions.connect(id)`; send one `world_setup` (text and/or a 16:9 `reference_image` with only `mime_type` + `data`; adding `type` is rejected), then `user_action` key_down/key_up with key_w/a/s/d and arrow_up/down/left/right, or plain text events. Connect 0.5 s, first frame 2.9 s (text seed) / 4.9 s (image seed), 24 fps of 1280x720 JPEG frames plus 48 kHz stereo PCM. Seeded with a real ego2act frame it kept the scene and invented a hob when panned left. Max 120 s per session; call `complete()` exactly once (a second call returns "Precondition check failed"). The SDK marks `interactions.connect` experimental.
